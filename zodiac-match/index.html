Zodiac Match

Match 3 animal friends!

Tap tiles with the twelve zodiac animals. Put three of the same animal in the tray and they pop. Clear the board to win. Calm, colorful and made for kids.

Coming soon on Google Play

How to play

Tap a tile. It jumps into the tray. Three of a kind and pop!

What's inside

Lots to play, nothing to worry about.

117 levels

Easy, Medium and Hard sets, a Lunar New Year set, and three extra worlds with new animals.

Pick your challenge

Easy gives a roomy tray of 7 slots. Hard gives only 5, so you think ahead.

Daily puzzle

A new puzzle every day. Collect a stamp for each day in a row.

Sticker book

24 animal stickers to earn, and tile styles to unlock as you play.

10 languages

The game can say each animal's name out loud, in your language.

Color help

Extra marks on tiles for kids who see colors differently. Undo and shuffle when you get stuck.

Zodiac Match is built for young players. It works offline and keeps progress only on the device.
