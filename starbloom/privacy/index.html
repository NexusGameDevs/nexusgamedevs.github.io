Starbloom

Privacy policy

Starbloom by Nexus Game Development. Effective October 31, 2026.

Starbloom is a team battle game. It was made so that it never needs to know anything about the person playing it. This page explains what the game does and does not do with information.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for a name, email address, age, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information. There is no account and no sign in.

What stays on the device

The game saves your heroes, their levels, your stage progress, the gold and gems you have earned, and your settings in a file on the phone or tablet, with a backup copy beside it in case the first is damaged. These files are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Permissions

The game asks for no special device permissions. It does not use the camera, the microphone, contacts or location.

Internet, ads and analytics

The game plays fully offline and does not connect to the internet. It contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers and no social features. Players cannot chat with or contact anyone.

Purchases

The game has no purchases. Gold and gems are earned only by playing, and new heroes and items are bought only with them, never with money.

Children

Because we collect no personal information, we do not collect personal information from children under 13 or any other age. This meets the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families policy.

Changes

If a future version of the game changes any of this, for example by adding ads or purchases, we will update this page first and change the date at the top.
